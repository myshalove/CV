# Run a final review

Review the resume in four passes.

First, verify facts. Check every date, title, employer, degree, metric, link, and technology against your records.

Second, check relevance. Remove bullets that do not help the target hiring decision. Move the strongest relevant evidence upward.

Third, check language. Read each bullet aloud. Cut filler, repeated openings, unexplained acronyms, and unsupported adjectives. Keep verb tense consistent: present tense for current work and past tense for completed work.

Fourth, test the file:

1. Compile or export the PDF.
2. Confirm it is one or two pages.
3. Copy all text from the PDF into a plain-text editor.
4. Check section order, dates, and contact details.
5. Open every link.
6. Inspect the pages at normal zoom for clipping and awkward breaks.

Ask one trusted reviewer to explain what role they think the resume targets after a 20-second scan. If their answer is wrong, the opening needs work.
