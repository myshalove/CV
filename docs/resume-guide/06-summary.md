# Write a useful summary

A summary earns its place when it helps a reader classify you quickly. Keep it to three or four lines.

Include:

- profession and level;
- relevant years of experience when the count is defensible;
- one or two areas of strength;
- a result or scope that distinguishes you.

Developer:

> Backend engineer with six years of experience in data and commerce systems. Built event-driven services processing 11 million records per day and reduced reporting API latency by 66\%. Strong in Go, Python, PostgreSQL, and production observability.

Designer:

> Product designer focused on operational software. Led research and interaction design for dispatch tools used by 1,200 people, reducing exception resolution time by 27\%. Experienced in accessible design systems and complex workflows.

Operations:

> Regional operations manager with eight years in food distribution. Managed three facilities and a \$12 million budget while improving on-time dispatch from 89\% to 96\%.

Avoid objectives about what you hope to receive, lists of soft skills, and claims such as "results-oriented visionary." Let the evidence carry the message.
